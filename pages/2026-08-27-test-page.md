<p>test content</p><p>Mike Furlough, HathiTrust’s Executive Director, said, “For decades, libraries have sustained their values in the face of rapid and powerful advances in technology. Libraries created HathiTrust to ensure that the results of mass digitization would address the unique needs of researchers and the academy. This project continues that longstanding effort to collaboratively make the scholarly record more accessible and usable for today and into the future.”</p><p><em>Transparent Books</em> also builds on the 2024–2026 <a href="https://authorsalliance.substack.com/p/the-public-interest-corpus-final">Public Interest Corpus project</a>, led by Authors Alliance and Northeastern University, which examined how research libraries can make book data available for computational research in lawful ways that serve the public interest rather than reinforce commercial control of large-scale digital collections. Through <em>Transparent Books</em>, HathiTrust will use the framework identified in the Public Interest Corpus project to deliver its corpus for noncommercial AI research and development.</p><p>Authors Alliance Executive Director, Dave Hansen, shared, “Our role in <em>Transparent Books</em> is to help articulate principles for use of the corpus, convene the authors, researchers, and institutions the project depends on so their views can be meaningfully reflected in the project’s designs. Ultimately, we want to lower barriers for noncommercial, academic researchers so they can build models and tools that actually advance research and benefit the public. And we want to see this happen in a way that ensures authors’ works and ideas are discovered, attributed, and built upon.</p>
