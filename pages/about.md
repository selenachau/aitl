The Acquisitions Institute at Timberline Lodge is a library conference that has been providing an opportunity for acquisitions and collection development for librarians, vendors and publishers to meet and discuss pertinent trends and issues in libraries since 2000.

For more information on the history of the Acquisitions Institute at Timberline Lodge, please refer to the ++[Acquisitions Institute History](https://acquisitionsinstitute.org/history/)++ page, as well as the following reports:
